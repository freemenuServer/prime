How this version works

The 56 rules are split into 12 trend, 11 momentum, 11 volatility, 11 volume and 11 price-action votes. Each returns +1 / 0 / −1; the master score is bull count minus bear count, with the requested ±35 threshold. Binance crypto candles update on closed bars. For XAU/USD/EUR/USD, only CSV is connected. CVD/flow and Volume Profile are OHLCV estimates; true DOM, bid/ask delta, footprint, verified calendar, and Guardeer’s private weights/API are not connected.

No live trade execution. Strong signals are local calculations, not Guardeer Prime output.
Model settings (local approximation)
The +35 / −35 threshold is user-requested. Rule definitions are local implementations and are not Guardeer’s proprietary formulas. True DOM and footprint rules remain neutral without those data feeds.
